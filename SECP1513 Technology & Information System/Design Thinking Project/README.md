## REFLECTION 
*&This project was a long yet highly engaging journey. My teammate and I collaborated closely to develop an idea aimed at addressing a real-world problem, with the main objective of supporting individuals affected by Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). Initially, we overlooked the practical limitations and realistic challenges regarding the ability of autistic children to directly use a therapeutic application to express their emotions. However, through deeper reflection and discussion, we recognized that parents are often the most significantly affected group, as they face numerous challenges—especially when ASD is not widely recognized or well understood within the community. This lack of awareness can make the process of raising a child with ASD particularly demanding.

Based on this realization, we developed a prototype designed to present visual content to users and gather their feedback, enabling parents to better understand and support their children. For me, this project was a valuable and meaningful experience. It provided an opportunity for students to think critically, apply logical reasoning, and explore creative yet practical solutions to contemporary issues. The process of designing, developing, and completing this project marked a significant new chapter in my personal and academic journey. &*
