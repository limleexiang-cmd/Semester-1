## REFLECTION 
This project was a long yet highly engaging journey. My teammate and I worked closely to develop an idea aimed at addressing a real-world problem, with the primary objective of supporting individuals affected by Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). At the initial stage, we overlooked certain practical limitations and realistic challenges, particularly regarding the ability of autistic children to directly use a therapeutic application to express their emotions.

However, through deeper reflection, discussion, and the design thinking process, we came to realize that parents are often the most significantly affected group. They face numerous challenges, especially when ASD is not widely recognized or well understood within the community. This lack of awareness can make the process of raising and supporting a child with ASD more demanding and emotionally challenging.

Based on this insight, we developed a prototype focused on presenting visual content and collecting user feedback, enabling parents to better understand their children’s emotional responses and needs. This project was a valuable and meaningful learning experience for me, as it encouraged critical thinking, logical reasoning, empathy, and creativity in problem-solving. The process of designing, developing, and completing this project marked a significant milestone in my personal and academic journey, and it reinforced the importance of user-centered design in creating impactful and socially responsible solutions.
