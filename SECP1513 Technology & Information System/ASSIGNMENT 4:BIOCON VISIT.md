# Industrial Visit :Biocon Sdn Bhd
DATE : 24 DECEMBER 2025

VENUE : Kawasan Perindustrian SILC, 1, Jln Bioteknologi 1, 79200 Iskandar Puteri, Johor
</div>
<div align="left">       

## REFLECTION 
*On 24 December 2025, I had the opportunity to visit Biocon, a leading global biopharmaceutical company headquartered in India with a branch operating in Malaysia. This industrial visit was a valuable and enriching experience, as it allowed us to tour the company and gain firsthand exposure to a real-world pharmaceutical production environment.*

*During the visit, the speakers delivered insightful and meaningful talks that encouraged us to reflect on the true purpose of technology. Although the concept may appear simple, it is often overlooked—technology is designed to support and assist humanity, not to replace or overpower it, but to help achieve goals more efficiently and effectively. This message left a strong impression on me and emphasized the ethical responsibility that accompanies technological advancement, especially in the healthcare sector.*

*In addition, the visit provided valuable insights into the insulin production process, particularly the duplication and manufacturing of insulin using advanced biotechnological methods. The session introduced many new concepts that I was previously unfamiliar with, making the experience both educational and inspiring. Overall, this industrial visit broadened my understanding of how technology and biotechnology are applied in the healthcare industry to improve patient outcomes and enhance quality of life.*
